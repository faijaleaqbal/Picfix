Frequently Asked Questions

Does compression reduce image quality?

We use smart lossy compression techniques to selectively decrease the number of colors in the image data. The effect is nearly invisible but it makes a very large difference in file size.

Are my images secure?

Yes. All processing is done locally in your browser. Your images are never uploaded to our servers, ensuring complete privacy and security.
