Frequently Asked Questions

Does cropping reduce image quality?

Cropping removes pixels, which technically reduces the overall resolution, but it does not degrade the quality of the remaining pixels. It simply changes the framing.

Can I crop to a specific size in inches or cm?

Currently, our tool operates in pixels. To achieve a specific print size, crop to the desired aspect ratio first, then use our Resize tool to adjust the DPI and print dimensions.
